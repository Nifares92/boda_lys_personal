Everything you need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical information so you can simply focus on enjoying the celebration to the fullest.

🚕

How do we get around? (No shuttle buses)

There is no private shuttle bus service, so transfers should be done by private vehicle or taxi/rideshare. The drive between Alicante Town Hall and Finca Santa Luzia takes just 15–18 minutes.

Radio Tele Taxi Alicante: +34 965 25 25 11

Rideshare apps available in Alicante: Uber, Cabify, Bolt, and Free Now.

🅿️

Where can we park?

At the Town Hall: We recommend nearby public parking garages (such as Parking Puerto, San Cristóbal, or Alfonso el Sabio).

At Finca Santa Luzia: The venue's on-site parking is somewhat limited (about 25 to 40 cars). There is street parking nearby, but we warmly suggest carpooling or using a taxi where possible.

👠

What footwear should we wear? (Lawn notice)

The welcome cocktail will take place outdoors on lawn grass. For those wearing stiletto/thin heels, we will have heel protector stoppers available so you don't sink in. Block heels, wedges, or flats will have no trouble at all.

🌙

What happens after midnight (00:00)?

The party at Finca Santa Luzia concludes at midnight, but for those who feel like continuing to dance and drink, Alicante has plenty of great nightlife! We can move together to nearby clubs like Texaco, Copity, Magma, or Marmarela. Options for everyone wanting to extend the night!

👔

Dress Code

Formal / Cocktail & party attire. You have full freedom with colors (the grooms are still deciding whether to wear olive green or not!). The only essential rule is to come elegant, comfortable, and ready to celebrate.

🥗

Allergies or Special Menus

The entire menu will be fully catered to celiacs, vegetarians, vegans, lactose-intolerant, or any other allergies. We just kindly ask you to specify it in the RSVP form before December 5, 2026.
